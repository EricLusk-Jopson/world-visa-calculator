## What changed and why

The app capped visa-free stays under Belarus's airport scheme (76
nationalities, 30 days per visit through six airports) at 90 days per
calendar year in total. No MFA page states that cap for this scheme. The
airport page gives "up to 30 days" and says the stay can't be extended. The
general visa-free page gives "up to 30 days for citizens of 76 countries".
Only the European any-border scheme states a 90-days-per-calendar-year
total. So the cap is gone from the airport tier, which is now 30 days per
visit with unlimited visits. The European override (until 31 December 2026)
keeps it.

This affects the 26 airport-only nationalities, and the 38 European
override nationalities once the override ends and they fall back to the
airport tier (trips from 2027). The 12 conditional nationalities are
modelled as visa-required, so they're unaffected.

This corrects the transcription. The source didn't change; the maintainer
decided to drop the cap after the 2026-10-23 check found no source for it.

## Evidence

Airport page (`airport`, "Information on visa-free travel via the airports
of the Republic of Belarus"):

> foreign citizens of 76 countries can enter Belarus for up to 30 days* and
> exit from the territory of Belarus only through the State border
> checkpoints [...]

> The term of visa-free stay can not be extended beyond the established 30
> days.

European-countries page (`europe`), the only page stating a yearly total,
for that scheme:

> Travellers may make use of this visa-free regime an unlimited number of
> times until December 31, 2026 inclusive. The total duration of visa-free
> stays must not exceed 90 days within a calendar year.

## Checked

The maintainer read the airport, European-countries and general
visa-free pages (https://mfa.gov.by/en/visa/freemove/general/) on the live
site on 2026-10-23, along with the airport PDF (76 countries, matching
`belarus.ts`). None of them states a yearly cap for the airport scheme.
`lastVerified` in `belarus.ts` was bumped to 2026-10-23, and so was
`dateChecked` on the airport and European sources.
