**Earliest entry date: December 31, 2025** (the day right after Trip 4 ends).

**Reasoning**

A 21-day trip starting Dec 31, 2025 ends Jan 20, 2026. Because each new day adds one day to the count while the window can drop at most one old day, the count never decreases during the trip. So it's enough to check the final day, Jan 20, 2026.

The 180-day window ending Jan 20, 2026 starts on July 25, 2025. Counting days (inclusive) from Jordan's history:

- Trip 1 (Mar 14–21): 8 days, before the window, so not counted.
- Trip 2 (Apr 18 – Jul 2): 76 days, ended July 2, before the window, so not counted.
- Trip 3 (Sep 19–26): 8 days, in the window.
- Trip 4 (Nov 27 – Dec 30): 34 days, in the window.

That gives 42 prior days in the window. Adding the 21 new days gives 63, which is under the 90-day limit. The same holds on every earlier day of the trip, since the window then reaches back to dates that include even fewer prior days.

Jordan's heavy Trip 2 usage has already aged out of the 180-day window by the end of 2025, so no waiting period is needed.
