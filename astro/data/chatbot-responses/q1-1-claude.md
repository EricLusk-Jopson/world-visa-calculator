**August 28, 2025**

**Her old trip used the full allowance.** March 1 to May 29, 2025 (counting both days) is 31 + 30 + 29 = 90 days.

**A full 90-day stay needs a window with none of those days.** The rule looks at the 180 days ending on each day of the stay. If a new 90-day stay is to end with exactly 90 days used, none of the old 90 days can be in the window on its last day. If any were, the total would go over 90.

**Working out the start date.** For a stay starting on day D, the last day is D + 89. Its 180-day window begins 90 days before D. That window start must be after May 29, so it must be May 30 or later. Counting 90 days forward from May 30 gives August 28.

**Check.** A stay from August 28 to November 25, 2025 has a window on its last day that starts May 30, which contains none of her March–May days. So the total is exactly 90.

Starting earlier would be possible, but only for fewer days. Her old days drop out one at a time starting March 2 (the old trip begins leaving the window on August 28). For example, on August 28 the window still holds 89 old days, so only 1 day would be allowed.
