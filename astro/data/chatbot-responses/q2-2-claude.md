**Earliest entry date: June 21, 2025**

**Reasoning:**

- Anna's earlier stay (Feb 1 to Mar 22, 2025, inclusive) used 50 days.
- A 60-day trip uses 60 more. Together that is 110, so at least 20 of the old days must fall out of the rolling 180-day window by the final day of the new trip. Otherwise she exceeds 90.
- Her count never goes down as the trip goes on, because the window drops at most one old day for each new day added. So the last day of the trip is the binding day.
- The first 20 old days (Feb 1 to Feb 20) must be outside the window on that last day. The window must therefore start no earlier than Feb 21, 2025, which leaves 30 old days (Feb 21 to Mar 22) plus 60 new days, exactly 90.
- A 180-day window ending on day D starts on D − 179. Setting D − 179 = Feb 21, 2025 gives D = Aug 19, 2025 as the earliest possible last day.
- Counting back 60 days inclusive from Aug 19 gives an entry date of June 21, 2025. The stay runs June 21 to Aug 19.
