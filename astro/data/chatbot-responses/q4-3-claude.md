Ilker's earlier stay (Feb 1 to Mar 22, 2025) is 50 days, counting both the entry and exit days. The 90/180 rule is checked on every day of a stay, by counting the days spent in Schengen within the 180 days ending that day.

- **Aug 1, 2025 (entry day):** Aug 1 is 181 days after Feb 1, so the 180-day window runs from Feb 3 to Aug 1. That window still holds 48 days of the old stay (Feb 3 to Mar 22) plus the new day, for 49 total.
- **Aug 2 to Sep 18:** Each new day adds one day to the count, but the window also drops one old day. The total stays at 49 until the last old day falls out of the window on Sep 18.
- **Sep 19 onward:** The old stay no longer counts, so the total is just the days of the current trip, rising by one each day.
- **Day 90 (Oct 29, 2025):** The window (May 3 to Oct 29) contains only the new trip, for exactly 90 days. That is the maximum allowed, so he has not overstayed.

He would need to leave by Oct 29, because a 91st day would put him over the limit.
