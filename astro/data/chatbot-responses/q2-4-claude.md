**Earliest entry date: January 29, 2026** (the 50-day stay would run through March 19, 2026).

**Days used in each earlier trip (counting both the entry and exit days):**

- Trip 1 (Mar 14–21, 2025): 8 days
- Trip 2 (Apr 18 – Jul 2, 2025): 76 days
- Trip 3 (Sep 19–26, 2025): 8 days
- Trip 4 (Nov 27 – Dec 30, 2025): 34 days

**Reasoning:**

- By December 31, 2025, the 180-day window no longer reaches back to Trip 2, so Trips 1 and 2 stop counting. Only Trip 3 (8 days) and Trip 4 (34 days) remain, which is 42 days.
- A new 50-day stay would therefore add 50 days to those 42, giving 92 if Trip 3 were still in the window. That is over the limit, so Trip 3 must have partly rolled off by the last day of the stay. Trip 4 stays in the window throughout.
- The count rises during the stay (new days are added faster than old ones drop out), so the binding day is the last one. On that day the total is 50 + 34 + (Trip 3 days still inside the window) ≤ 90. That means at most 6 days of Trip 3 may remain, which are Sept 21–26.
- On March 19, 2026 the window starts on September 21, 2025, so exactly those 6 days remain. The total is 50 + 34 + 6 = 90, which is the maximum allowed.
- A stay ending one day earlier would include 7 days of Trip 3 and reach 91, which is an overstay. So the stay must end no earlier than March 19, which puts the earliest entry 49 days before, on January 29, 2026.
