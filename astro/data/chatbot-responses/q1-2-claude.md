**June 21, 2025**, and the date is the same for all four friends because they traveled together.

**Reasoning:**

- The trip from February 1 to March 22, 2025 (inclusive) is 28 + 22 = 50 days.
- A full 90-day stay beginning June 21 runs through September 18, 2025.
- The rule is checked on every day of the stay, looking back over the 180 days ending that day (including that day). The binding check is the last day of the stay, since that is when the window reaches furthest back toward the old trip.
- On September 18, the 180-day window starts on March 23, 2025, so it just misses March 22. The 50 earlier days are all outside it, leaving 90 days in the window, exactly the limit.
- If they started even one day earlier (June 20), the stay would end September 17. That window would begin March 22, which catches one day of the old trip and gives 91 days, a violation.

They could enter earlier for a shorter stay, since their 50 days only gradually drop out of the window. But June 21, 2025 is the first start date that allows the full 90 days.
