Patricia used exactly **90 days** from March 1 through May 29, 2025, inclusive.

To make a **brand-new 90-day stay**, she needs all 90 days of that new stay to fit within the 90-day allowance in every applicable rolling 180-day window. If she starts on August 28, her 90th day would be **November 25**. On November 25, the relevant 180-day window begins **May 30**, so her entire earlier trip (which ended May 29) has dropped out of the calculation.

Therefore:

**Earliest possible new 90-day entry: August 28, 2025.**

This is later than the often-assumed “90 days after leaving” date because the rule is based on a **rolling 180-day window**, and a full new 90-day stay requires the old 90 days to have completely fallen outside the relevant window.
