Patricia's first trip (March 1 to May 29, 2025, inclusive) used exactly 90 days. The 180-day window ending on any given day covers that day and the 179 days before it.

- **Aug 28, 2025 (day 1 of the new trip):** The window runs from March 2 to August 28. March 1 has already dropped out, leaving 89 days from the old trip (March 2 to May 29). Adding Aug 28 makes 90 days, which is allowed.
- **Each later day:** One old day drops out of the window and one new day is added, so the total stays at exactly 90. On day _k_ of the stay, the old trip contributes 90 − _k_ days and the new trip contributes _k_ days.
- **Nov 25, 2025 (day 90):** The last old day (May 29) drops out of the window, and the 90 days counted are all from the new trip.

She is at the 90-day limit every day of the stay but never over it, so she can stay from August 28 through November 25. She must leave that day, since staying a day longer would exceed 90 days.
