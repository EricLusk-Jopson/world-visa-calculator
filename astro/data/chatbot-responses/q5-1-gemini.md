The earliest date Anna can join Marcus and Ilker for a 7-day trip is **September 2, 2025**.

**Key Rules & Requirements**

1. **The Target Window:** Marcus and Ilker are both in the Schengen Area together between **August 1, 2025** and **October 12, 2025** (the end of Marcus's stay).
2. **The 90/180 Rule:** On _every single day_ of Anna's trip, her total days inside the Schengen Area during the preceding 180-day lookback period (including that day) cannot exceed **90 days**.

---

**Anna's Travel Timeline in 2025**

| Trip           | Dates          | Days Spent  |
| -------------- | -------------- | ----------- |
| **Trip 1**     | Feb 1 – Mar 22 | 50 days     |
| **Trip 2**     | May 3 – Jun 11 | 40 days     |
| **Total Used** |                | **90 days** |

By June 11, 2025, Anna had fully used her 90-day allowance. To re-enter without overstaying, she must wait until her earlier travel days fall outside her rolling 180-day window.

**Calculating the Earliest Start Date**

For a 7-day trip starting on date D and ending on date D+6:

1. **Checking August 1:** On August 1, 2025, looking back 180 days covers **February 3 to August 1**. The 50-day first trip (Feb 1 – Mar 22) is almost entirely within this lookback window, so Anna has no available days remaining.
2. **Rolling Off Trip 1:** The last day of Anna's first trip (March 22) falls outside the 180-day window on **September 18, 2025** (March 22 + 180 days).
3. **Reclaiming Days:**

- Starting **August 1, 2025**, each day Anna waits drops 1 day of her February trip from the 180-day lookback, earning her 1 available day.
- By **August 27, 2025**, 27 days of her February trip have dropped off, leaving her with 27 available days.

4. **Accounting for 7 Continuous Days:**

- If Anna enters on **September 2, 2025**, her trip runs from **Sept 2 through Sept 8**.
- On **Sept 8** (the final day of the trip), the 180-day lookback covers **March 13 to Sept 8**.
- During this lookback period, Anna's past travel consists of March 13–22 (10 days) plus May 3–June 11 (40 days) = **50 days**.
- Adding her new 7-day trip brings her 180-day total to **57 days**, which is safely below the 90-day limit.

Thus, **September 2, 2025** is the earliest date Anna can enter to stay for 7 continuous days with Marcus and Ilker.
