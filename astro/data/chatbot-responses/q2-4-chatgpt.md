The earliest possible entry date is **January 29, 2026**.

Reasoning:

- Schengen’s 90/180 rule counts **every day of presence**, including entry and exit days.
- A 50-day new trip therefore requires that, at every point during the trip, no more than **40 days of prior Schengen presence** fall within the preceding 180 days.
- For an entry on **January 29, 2026**, the 50-day trip runs through **March 19, 2026**.
- On the final day, March 19, the relevant 180-day window contains exactly **40 days from Jordan’s previous trips**, plus the **50 days of the new trip = 90 days**.
- Entering even one day earlier, **January 28**, would result in 91 days within the rolling 180-day window at a point during the trip.

**Answer: January 29, 2026.**
