Patricia's first trip, March 1 to May 29, 2025, covers 90 days (31 in March, 30 in April, 29 in May), so she used her entire allowance.

On August 20, 2025, the 180-day window runs from February 22 to August 20, which includes every one of those 90 days. She would already be at 90 days on arrival, so she couldn't enter without overstaying, let alone stay a further 90 days.

Her days only free up gradually as the old ones roll out of the window. To get a full fresh 90 days with no overlap, the entire old trip must be outside the window. That means the window start must be after May 29. The earliest such entry date is **November 25, 2025**, when the window runs May 30 to November 25. A 90-day stay from then would stay compliant throughout.
