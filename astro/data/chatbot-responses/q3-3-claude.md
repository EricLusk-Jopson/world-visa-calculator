**October 12, 2025**

**Reasoning:**

- The February 1 to March 22 stay counts as 50 days, inclusive of both dates.
- Marcus enters July 15. The old 50 days stay in the lookback window for a while, but they roll off gradually starting July 31, when the window moves past February 1. They are all gone from the window by about September 18.
- From July 15 until the old days have fully rolled off, his running total stays comfortably under 90. It peaks at about 66 days, because each new day in the Schengen Area is offset by an old day dropping out.
- Once the old trip is out of the window (September 18 onward), only the new stay counts. His 90th consecutive day of the new stay, counting July 15 as day 1, is October 12, 2025. At that point the 180-day window (April 16 to October 12) contains exactly 90 days, all from this stay.
- Being present on October 13 would be day 91, which exceeds the limit. He must be out by the end of October 12.

So the earlier trip has no effect on the result: he can stay a full 90 days.
